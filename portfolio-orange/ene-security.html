ENE Security / 2023–2026

MarketingLead.

I led marketing strategy and sales enablement for a New England commercial security integrator, managing the brand across website, SEO, social, trade shows, customer events, partner campaigns, proposals, collateral, presentations, and customer stories.

RoleMarketing Lead + Sales Support
IndustryCommercial Security / B2B Technology
LocationsSalem, NH / Canton, MA
ScopeStrategy through execution

01 / The role

One role.
Every channel.

ENE was the kind of position where “marketing” meant owning the entire system—not one channel.

I worked between leadership, sales, customers, technology manufacturers, and outside partners to turn technical security solutions into marketing that people could actually understand and act on.

That meant creating the visual work, writing and organizing the message, supporting the sales process, maintaining the website and SEO, planning events, producing trade-show experiences, building co-branded campaigns, and finding better ways to tell the story of completed customer work.

02 / Scope

What I
owned.

The role combined design craft with practical marketing leadership. Every piece had to strengthen ENE's brand while also helping sales conversations move forward.
