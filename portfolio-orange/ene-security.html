MarketingLead.

Brand / demand / events / sales enablement

I led ENE Security's in-house marketing while supporting sales—owning the brand across design, website, SEO, social, trade shows, customer events, partner campaigns, proposals, collateral, presentations, and customer stories.

IndustryCommercial Security / B2B Technology

01 / The role

One role.
Every channel.

ENE was a true full-stack marketing role: design the work, build the campaign, support sales, run the event, update the website, and make the brand feel consistent everywhere.

I worked between leadership, sales, customers, and technology manufacturers to turn complex security solutions into clear, useful marketing. The goal was never just to make something look good—it had to explain the product, help a salesperson, strengthen a partner relationship, or create a reason for a customer to engage.
